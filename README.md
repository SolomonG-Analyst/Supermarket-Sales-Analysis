# 🛒 Supermarket Sales Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This analysis examines supermarket sales data from 2021 and 2022, covering 5 categories and 45 products. **Excel** was used for data cleaning, and **Power BI** was employed for building an interactive dashboard to enhance interpretation and business insight.

## 🎯 Objective
The objective of this analysis is to:
- Identify sales trends
- Evaluate product and category performance
- Provide actionable insights to optimize sales strategies and improve profitability

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Sales Trends**: Sales increased significantly from 2021 to 2022, with the lowest sales in March 2021 and April 2022.
- **Top Products**: Product 41 achieved the highest sales at $23K, though no product dominated the market.
- **Profit**: Profit rose from $30K in 2021 to $39K in 2022.
- **Sales Type**: Direct sales were the highest, followed by online sales, while wholesaler sales were the lowest.
- **Payment Mode**: Cash was the most popular payment method, surpassing digital modes.
- **Category Performance**: Category03 had the lowest sales, with other categories performing relatively equally.

## 💡 Recommendations
1. **Seasonal Promotions**: Target low-sales months (March and April) with discounts or campaigns.
2. **Enhance Online Sales**: Improve online shopping experience with promotions and better marketing.
3. **Inventory Management**: Align stock levels with product performance to prevent over/understocking.
4. **Boost Wholesaler Engagement**: Offer special deals or incentives to increase wholesaler contribution.
5. **Promote Digital Payments**: Encourage digital payments through loyalty points or exclusive offers.

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies
- **Excel** – For data cleaning and preparation
- **Power BI** – For dashboard development and data visualization
